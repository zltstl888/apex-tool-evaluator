# Community conduct

APEX Tool Evaluator welcomes evidence, counterexamples, and respectful disagreement. Participation should make tool-adoption decisions easier to inspect and safer to challenge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and curious about different operating contexts.
- Critique claims, rules, tests, and evidence without attacking people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest, affiliations, or promotional relationships.
- Use public or synthetic examples and remove credentials, customer data, private paths, and confidential identifiers.
- Accept a maintainer's request to narrow, redact, or move security-sensitive material to a private channel.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of confidential information, undisclosed spam, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary. Use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for abusive content. Report vulnerabilities privately as described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); do not post exploit details in a conduct report.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
